# MBTI Types Reference

A quick reference for the 16 Myers-Briggs Type Indicator personality types.

## The Four Dichotomies

- **E/I** — Extraversion vs. Introversion (where you get energy)
- **S/N** — Sensing vs. Intuition (how you process information)
- **T/F** — Thinking vs. Feeling (how you make decisions)
- **J/P** — Judging vs. Perceiving (how you approach structure)

## The 16 Types

### Analysts (NT)

| Type | Stack | Traits |
|------|-------|--------|
| **INTJ** | Ni-Te-Fi-Se | Strategic thinker, loves systems and long-term planning. Private, decisive, high standards. Weakness: can seem cold or dismissive of emotions. |
| **INTP** | Ti-Ne-Si-Fe | Logical explorer, loves ideas for their own sake. Flexible, objective, independent. Weakness: analysis paralysis, can neglect practical matters. |
| **ENTJ** | Te-Ni-Se-Fi | Natural leader, bold and goal-oriented. Efficient, charismatic, confronts problems head-on. Weakness: can be domineering or impatient with feelings. |
| **ENTP** | Ne-Ti-Fe-Si | Idea generator, loves debate and possibilities. Quick-witted, curious, non-conformist. Weakness: can argue for sport, struggles with follow-through. |

### Diplomats (NF)

| Type | Stack | Traits |
|------|-------|--------|
| **INFJ** | Ni-Fe-Ti-Se | Quiet visionary, deeply intuitive about people. Idealistic, creative, seeks meaning. Weakness: perfectionist, prone to burnout from caring too much. |
| **INFP** | Fi-Ne-Si-Te | Guided by inner values, seeks authenticity and beauty. Empathetic, creative, open-minded. Weakness: overly idealistic, can withdraw when hurt. |
| **ENFJ** | Fe-Ni-Se-Ti | People-focused leader, warm and inspiring. Sees potential in others, excellent communicator. Weakness: can neglect own needs, too focused on harmony. |
| **ENFP** | Ne-Fi-Te-Si | Enthusiastic explorer of people and ideas. Spontaneous, warm, sees connections everywhere. Weakness: easily distracted, can overcommit. |

### Sentinels (SJ)

| Type | Stack | Traits |
|------|-------|--------|
| **ISTJ** | Si-Te-Fi-Ne | Dependable, fact-based, follows through. Values tradition, order, and responsibility. Weakness: can be rigid, slow to accept change. |
| **ISFJ** | Si-Fe-Ti-Ne | Quiet protector, warm and diligent. Remembers details about people, service-oriented. Weakness: avoids conflict, can be taken for granted. |
| **ESTJ** | Te-Si-Ne-Fi | Efficient organizer, values rules and results. Direct, practical, dependable. Weakness: can be blunt, struggles with unconventional ideas. |
| **ESFJ** | Fe-Si-Ne-Ti | Social caretaker, builds community and harmony. Attentive to others' needs, practical help. Weakness: seeks validation, can be gossipy under stress. |

### Explorers (SP)

| Type | Stack | Traits |
|------|-------|--------|
| **ISTP** | Ti-Se-Ni-Fe | Hands-on problem solver, cool under pressure. Adaptable, observant, self-reliant. Weakness: can seem detached, risk-taking streak. |
| **ISFP** | Fi-Se-Ni-Te | Gentle artist, lives in the moment. Aesthetic, kind, quietly adventurous. Weakness: avoids conflict, can be overly sensitive. |
| **ESTP** | Se-Ti-Fe-Ni | Action-oriented, thrives on excitement and risk. Bold, persuasive, reads situations quickly. Weakness: impatient, can push boundaries too far. |
| **ESFP** | Se-Fi-Te-Ni | Life of the party, brings energy and fun. Warm, observant, generous. Weakness: avoids deep planning, seeks immediate gratification. |

## Writing Character from MBTI

When writing dialogue and behavior for an OC with a given MBTI:

- **Cognitive functions** are more revealing than the four-letter code. For instance, an INFJ leads with Introverted Intuition (Ni), which means they naturally synthesize patterns and glimpse future implications — they might say things like "I have a feeling about this" without being able to articulate why immediately.
- **Under stress**, each type's inferior function emerges. An ENTP (inferior Si) might become obsessively detail-oriented when anxious. An ISFP (inferior Te) might suddenly become bossy and critical.
- **Growth arcs** often involve developing the inferior function. Use this for character development.
